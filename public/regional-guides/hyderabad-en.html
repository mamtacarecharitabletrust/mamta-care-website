How to donate hair in Hyderabad

Whether you live in Hyderabad, elsewhere in Telangana or in Andhra Pradesh, you can register with Mamta Care Charitable Trust and send your hair by courier to support wig making for people undergoing cancer treatment.

Register to donate hair

Before your haircut or head shave

Measure the length you intend to donate, from the band to the healthy ends. You need at least 14 usable inches, excluding split ends. Both a donation haircut and a full head shave are welcome.

Arranging collection in Hyderabad

Mamta Care offers doorstep hair donation in Hyderabad. To arrange a visit, give the team your locality and PIN code before booking. The team can confirm which options are available at your address. If you use your own salon, ask the stylist to tie the dry hair firmly before cutting so the strands stay together.

Registration questions? Call Mamta Care · 85000 12190

Sending hair from Telangana or Andhra Pradesh

  1. Register before the cut and keep your registration ID.
  2. Make sure the hair is clean and completely dry. Secure it tightly with a rubber band. Wrap it in paper or foil, seal it inside a plastic pouch and place it in an envelope.
  3. Write your return address on the envelope. Use the destination supplied by Mamta Care, and keep the courier receipt.

Submit courier details. Use your registration ID to share the tracking number and the requested donation photos or video.
