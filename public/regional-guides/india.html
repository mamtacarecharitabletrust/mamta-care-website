Donate hair from anywhere in India

Mamta Care Charitable Trust is a women-led NGO that collects donated hair across India for wigs for cancer patients. We offer doorstep collection in metropolitan cities. From other towns and villages, you can register and send your hair by courier.

Other metropolitan cities and the rest of India. For doorstep service in a metro, including Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai and Kolkata, contact the team to arrange a visit. Donors throughout northern and southern India can also use the courier option.

Call Mamta Care to arrange collection. If you need someone to perform the haircut or head shave, mention this when you call so the team can confirm the arrangements before your appointment.

Can I donate a haircut or a full head shave?

Both are welcome. Before cutting, measure from the rubber band to the healthy ends. The donation needs at least 14 usable inches, excluding split ends. For a head shave donation, ask the stylist to tie the long sections before shaving so the hair stays together.

Already cut your hair?

Keep the tied hair clean and completely dry. Wrap it in paper or foil, seal it in a plastic pouch and put the pouch in an envelope. Use the destination supplied by Mamta Care and include your return address. Keep the tracking number, then submit your courier details using your registration ID. For an existing donation, check its status.

If your hair is already cut and you are unsure whether it meets the requirements, call the team before sending it.
