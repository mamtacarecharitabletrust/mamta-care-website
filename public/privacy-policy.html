Privacy Policy

Mamta Care Charitable Trust is a hair donation organisation. This notice explains how we handle personal information received from donors, wig applicants and other people who contact us.

1. Information we collect

Depending on your interaction, we may collect your name, phone or WhatsApp number, city, optional email, hair length and type, donation preferences, front and back hair photographs, consent choices, registration ID, courier and collection details, correspondence, and donation progress. Other services, such as wig assistance or volunteering, may request additional details relevant to those services.

2. Why we use it

We use this information to receive and manage requests, coordinate hair donation and collection, issue registration confirmations and certificates, track donation progress, respond to questions, avoid duplicate applications, and improve our services. We use WhatsApp or email for relevant communications where permitted under applicable rules and consent choices.

3. Systems that process information

Our current and developing services may use our website, Cloudflare-hosted services, Google Forms, Google Sheets, Google email automation, and Meta WhatsApp. Photographs may be held in access-controlled application storage. Use of these third-party platforms is also subject to their own policies.

4. Who can access it

Access is limited to authorised personnel and relevant service providers involved in the request or donation process. We do not sell donor personal information. Data may be disclosed where required by law or with appropriate permission.

5. Donor photographs

Registration photographs are collected to help assess and coordinate a proposed hair donation. Submitting photos for registration does not automatically grant permission to publish your name, face, pictures or donation story on social media. Separate permission is required for public promotion.

6. Storage, security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to restrict access to personal records. We retain information for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, the administration of donations, legitimate recordkeeping, and applicable legal obligations. Retention may vary by record type.

7. Your choices

You can contact us to ask about, correct or request deletion of personal data, subject to identity verification and applicable retention obligations. You can also request that we stop optional WhatsApp or email communications. Please do not send sensitive identity documents through ordinary email unless we advise an appropriate secure verification process.

8. Privacy and deletion requests

See the Data Deletion Requests page for how to submit a request. We review requests before taking action so that someone else cannot delete or alter your donation record without authorisation.

9. Contact

Email bharthairseva.in@gmail.com. Please mention your registration ID, if available, to help us find the correct record.

10. Updates

We may update this policy when our systems, practices or legal requirements change. The date on this page identifies its current revision.
