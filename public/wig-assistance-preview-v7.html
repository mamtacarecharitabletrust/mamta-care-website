Important information · Please read before continuing

The application and wig have no purchase price. Please read the clear, written conditions before submitting.

  • Eligibility and review: Each request is reviewed individually. Submitting an application does not guarantee eligibility, wig availability, approval or immediate dispatch.
  • Personal use: Wigs are intended for the approved patient’s personal use and may not be resold or transferred without the Trust’s approval. Applicants should provide accurate details.
  • Payment and deposit: No booking fee or website payment is collected. Following verification and a phone discussion, a refundable security deposit may be required to safeguard the wig. The amount, conditions and applicable courier charges will be shared in writing before the patient decides whether to pay.
  • Wig return and refund: After the wig is returned and inspected under the agreed conditions, the security deposit will be refunded after deduction of previously disclosed applicable courier charges. Exact return arrangements, any other permitted deductions and refund timelines must be agreed in writing before payment.
  • Preparation and delivery: The estimated fulfillment period is approximately 45 working days after final approval and order confirmation, subject to fabrication, fitting, availability and delivery circumstances. Important delays will be communicated.
  • Privacy and patient media: Photos and WhatsApp selfie videos are used for verification and wig coordination. Awareness campaigns, educational material, training, and public social media use require separate optional consent. Declining publicity use does not automatically affect eligibility.
  • Support and rights: Mamta Care will communicate through its official channels. Applicants may ask questions about eligibility, deposits, shipping, refunds or concerns. Applicable legal rights remain unchanged.
