Privacy & transparency

Data Deletion Requests

If you registered to donate hair, requested assistance or contacted Mamta Care through our website or WhatsApp, you can request deletion of personal information we hold about you.

How to request deletion

  1. Use the email address associated with your registration if possible.
  2. Email bharthairseva.in@gmail.com with the subject Mamta Care — Data Deletion Request.
  3. Provide your registered name, registration ID if known, and which personal details or photographs you want reviewed for deletion. Add your registered phone number only if needed to identify the record.
  4. Our team will review the request, verify that you are authorised to make it, check any retention obligations, and communicate the outcome.
Prepare deletion request email

Important: This button opens a draft in your email application. Your request is received only after you send the email. Providing a registration ID alone is not sufficient authorisation to delete a record.

What we will review

Where relevant, we will identify matching personal information in the donor registration system, Google records, emails, related certificates, WhatsApp messages and stored photographs. Some information may need to be updated in multiple places.

Information that may need to be kept

We may need to keep limited records for applicable legal obligations, donation administration, fraud prevention or dispute resolution. When deletion cannot be completed, we will explain the applicable reason and consider minimising unnecessary identifiers.

Requests made for another person

We may require evidence that you are authorised to act for the individual concerned. Please do not send government identity-document photographs through an ordinary email without a secure verification arrangement.

Corrections or messaging preferences

You may use the same contact address to request correction of your record or to opt out of optional WhatsApp reminders.
